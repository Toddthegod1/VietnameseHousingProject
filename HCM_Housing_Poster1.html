Overview
Introduction

We analyze daily residential housing prices across nine Ho Chi Minh City districts (2017–2022), combining time-series modeling with exploratory data analysis and geographic context to understand what drives price differences between districts.

Following instructor feedback, this poster foregrounds the EDA findings — which are robust and novel — and treats the predictive modeling as explicitly exploratory.

Datasets
  • HousePricingHCM.csv — Daily prices, 9 districts, 1,363 obs. Core dataset.
  • consumerPriceIndex.csv — Vietnamese monthly CPI 1995–2024. Used to deflate nominal prices (2017 base).
  • populationData.csv — World Bank national population data for growth rate computation.
  • district_context.csv — District area (km²), population, coordinates. Used to compute density and CBD distance.

EDA - Growth
Real vs Nominal Growth
Fig 2. Nominal vs CPI-adjusted cumulative growth by district (2017 base)
⚠ Key Finding: Inflation erases gains in Districts 3 & 6

Districts 3 and 6 show 38–40% nominal growth but only 19–21% real growth — more than half their apparent appreciation was inflation. District 7's 379% nominal / 313% real gap is small by comparison, confirming genuine demand-driven appreciation.

Price Inequality

The absolute price gap grew from ~85M to ~147M VND (2017→2022). However, the coefficient of variation remained flat at 0.43–0.50, indicating relative inequality between districts is stable.

District 7 — Case Study
Why District 7 Outperformed
5.3 kmfrom CBD — close enough for urban spillover
10,091people/km² — lowest inner-district density
35.7 km²area — 4× larger than D3, D4, D5

Low starting density signaled large undeveloped land. Mid-range CBD distance attracted buyers priced out of the center. Growth from a low base on large land produces dramatic percentage appreciation — this is structural, not coincidental.

Conclusion: Geography is the strongest predictor

Distance to District 1 alone explains 76% of variance in average price across districts. Combined with population density, these two geographic variables explain both price levels and growth trajectories — and provide a structural account of District 7's exceptional performance.
